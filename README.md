# Clayface (2026)

## About the Movie
Clayface is a horror movie from DC Studios. It is about Matt Hagen, an actor who gets into an accident that changes his life.

## Movie Details
- **Title:** Clayface
- **Genre:** Horror, Thriller
- **Director:** James Watkins
- **Studio:** DC Studios

## Story
Matt Hagen is an actor who wants to fix his damaged face. He tries an experimental treatment, but something goes wrong. He gains the ability to change his appearance and eventually becomes Clayface.

## Why I Like It
I like this movie because the story is different from other DC movies. It focuses more on horror and how a person becomes a villain.
